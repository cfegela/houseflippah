# Game Rules

## General
- 1 to 4 players.
- Each player starts with **$100,000** in cash.
- Play proceeds sequentially by seat. Once all players finish their turn, the round advances.

## Buying Properties
- You can buy **1 property per round** from the bank.
- Properties require a **10% cash down payment**; the remaining 90% is financed into a 40-round mortgage.
- The mortgage payment for the round of purchase is covered automatically.

## Renovations
- Each property supports up to 5 improvements:
  1. *Windows & Doors*
  2. *HVAC & Mechanicals*
  3. *Roof & Siding*
  4. *Flooring & Painting*
  5. *Kitchen & Bathrooms*
- Each improvement costs **10% of the property's original purchase price**.
- Each improvement increases the property's market value by **20% of its purchase price** (up to 2x / +100% when fully upgraded).
- Each improvement can only be installed once per property.
- You cannot make improvements in the same round a property was purchased.
- You cannot make improvements while a property is actively listed for sale.
- You cannot make improvements in the same round a property was listed or delisted.

## Mortgages & Foreclosure
- Starting the round after purchase, each property requires a mortgage payment equal to **1/40th of its original purchase price** every round.
- Missing a payment marks the property as **delinquent** (1 missed payment).
- Missing a second consecutive payment results in **foreclosure**:
  - The defaulting owner loses the property.
  - The property returns to the bank with its mortgage cleared.
  - All installed improvements and elevated market value are preserved.
- A delinquent mortgage can be brought fully current with a double payment, or held off from foreclosure with a single payment.

## Listing Properties
- You **cannot list an unimproved property** (at least 1 improvement required).
- You cannot list a property in the same round it was purchased.
- You cannot list a property in the same round improvements were made to it.
- Asking price must be at least the remaining mortgage balance (maximum $10,000,000).
- Listed properties can be delisted at any time during your turn.

## Buyer Pool & Market Temperature
- At the end of each round, automated buyers evaluate all listed properties.
- Each round has a market temperature that sets the base odds of a property selling:
  - **Ice Cold**: 10% base odds
  - **Chilly**: 20% base odds
  - **Comfy**: 30% base odds
  - **Toasty**: 40% base odds
  - **On Fire**: 50% base odds
- Listing **at or below market value** receives full base odds.
- Listing **above market value** applies a pricing penalty that reduces sale odds.
- When a property sells:
  - The buyer pays the full asking price.
  - The outstanding mortgage balance is automatically paid off first.
  - Net profits are deposited directly into the player's cash on hand.
  - The sold property is permanently retired from the game.
